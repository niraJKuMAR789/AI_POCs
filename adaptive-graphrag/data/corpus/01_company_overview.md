# Helix Dynamics Company Overview

> Synthetic demo corpus. Helix Dynamics is a fictional company created to showcase multi-hop GraphRAG.

## Mission

Helix Dynamics builds autonomous robots for warehouse logistics and industrial inspection. The company
was founded in 2019 in Austin, Texas by Elena Vasquez (Chief Executive Officer) and Tomas Lindqvist
(Chief Technology Officer). Helix Dynamics employs roughly 420 people across Austin, Toronto and Pune.

## Products

Helix Dynamics sells three products:

- **Kestrel** is an autonomous mobile robot for warehouse picking and pallet transport.
- **Osprey** is an inspection drone for power lines, wind turbines and solar farms.
- **Heron** is the cloud fleet-management platform that dispatches, monitors and updates every Kestrel
  robot and Osprey drone in the field.

Kestrel is the largest revenue line, accounting for about 64 percent of 2025 revenue. Osprey launched
commercially in March 2025. Heron is bundled with every hardware subscription.

## Organization

Engineering is split into four groups that report to Tomas Lindqvist:

- The Perception group, led by Priya Raman, owns computer-vision models and on-robot inference.
- The Fleet Platform group, led by Marcus Chen, owns the Heron cloud platform.
- The Autonomy group, led by Kenji Watanabe, owns navigation, planning and motion control.
- The Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) group, led by Amara Okafor, owns observability,
  incident response and the on-call program.

The Applied AI team sits inside the Perception group and is led by Daniel Brooks. It builds the
generative-AI features in Heron, including the natural-language fleet assistant.

## Key customers

Major customers include Northgate Logistics, which operates 1,200 Kestrel robots across 14
warehouses, and Clearwater Energy, the first large Osprey customer, which inspects 3,000 km of
transmission lines per quarter.
